# Portrait

Drop a real photo of yourself at `/public/portrait.jpg`.

- Aspect ratio: 4:5 (e.g., 1200 × 1500)
- Will render in grayscale by default and turn full color on hover
- Format: JPG (smaller) or PNG (better quality)

Until you provide one, the About section shows a geometric SVG placeholder.

## Generation prompt (if you want an AI portrait)
> Cinematic editorial portrait of a young Nigerian-Canadian software engineering student, soft warm lighting, looking off-camera, minimal background, 4:5 ratio, film-grain, magazine cover quality

Save as `portrait.jpg` in this `/public/` folder.
